Field Deck No. 1  ·  A duel for two researchers

Travis:
The Game

Sixteen documented specimens of Travis Knox — the Ph.D., the Director, the Blue Suit, the Beer Frog — square off six-a-side in turn-based combat. Print it, cut it, play it at the kitchen table.

The Object

Each player fields a team of six Knoxes. Knock out every one of your opponent’s characters to win.

Setup

  1. Shuffle the 16 character cards and deal six to each player, face-down. Look at your own; keep them hidden from your opponent until each one takes its first turn.
  2. Track each character’s HP with coins, dice, or a pencil tally.
  3. Shuffle the 16 action cards into a draw pile. Each player draws two.

The Round

Every living character takes one turn per round, fastest first (highest SPD). Ties: roll a die.

On Your Character’s Turn

Draw one action card (unless you already hold three). Then do one thing:

  • Attack — deal damage equal to its ATK to one enemy.
  • Power — do what the card says. Each character’s Power works once per game.

You may also play one action card on each turn, before or after.

Damage & Knockouts

At 0 HP a character is knocked out: turn it face-down. Healing never goes above a character’s starting HP.

Two Words to Know

Shield — blocks all damage from the next hit, then it’s gone.
Skip — that character misses its next turn.

Shorter Games

Deal three or four characters each instead of six. Everything else stays the same.

Gift note. If a rule ever comes down to a coin flip, flip the coin. Travis will allow it.
Printing  ·  Ctrl/Cmd-P, Letter, margins .35in, scale 100%, background graphics ON. Four card pages of nine, plus a sheet of card backs — print the last page on the reverse of each card sheet (flip on long edge). Cut on the dashed lines.
