Field Deck No. 1  ·  A duel for two researchers

Travis:
The Game

Sixteen documented specimens of Travis Knox — the Ph.D., the Director, the Blue Suit, the Beer Frog — square off three-a-side in turn-based combat. Print it, cut it, play it at the kitchen table.

36 cards2 players20–30 min needs two d6 & coins for HPprint on cardstock, 4 pages + backs

The Object

Each researcher fields a team of three Knoxes. Knock out all three of your opponent’s specimens and the grant is yours.

Setup

  1. Lay the 16 character cards face-up in the middle. Roll a d6 — high roll drafts first, then alternate until each player has three. Unused characters go back in the box.
  2. Set each character’s HP with coins, dice, or a pencil tally on scrap paper.
  3. Shuffle the 19 action cards into a draw pile. Each player draws two.

The Round

A round is one turn for every living character, fastest first. Compare SPD across both teams and act in descending order. Ties: higher current HP goes first; still tied, roll a d6.

Turn order is recalculated at the start of every round, so buffs and knockouts change the batting order.

On Your Character’s Turn

Draw one action card (skip if the pile is empty), then take one of these:

  • Strike — deal damage equal to ATK to any one enemy.
  • Brace — heal 2 HP, and the next damage this character takes is reduced by 3.
  • Ability — use the card’s printed ability. Once per game unless the card says otherwise.

You may also play one action card per turn, before or after your action. Hand limit is 3 — discard down at end of turn.

Damage & Knockouts

Damage is subtracted from HP. At 0 or less, the character is knocked out: turn the card face-down. Knocked-out characters do not act, cannot be healed (unless a card brings them back), and cannot be targeted — but any printed “when knocked out” text still triggers.

Healing never exceeds a character’s printed starting HP. Damage is never reduced below 1 by stacking reductions.

Timing

“This round” effects end when the round ends. “Rest of the game” effects last until that character is knocked out. When two effects contradict, the one played most recently wins.

Longer Game: 4v4

For a longer game, each player drafts four Knoxes instead of three and starts with three action cards. Everything else stays the same.

Gift note. If a rule ever comes down to a coin flip, flip the coin. Travis will allow it.
Printing  ·  Ctrl/Cmd-P, Letter, margins .35in, scale 100%, background graphics ON. Four card pages of nine, plus a sheet of card backs — print the last page on the reverse of each card sheet (flip on long edge). Cut on the dashed lines.
